# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Flevio serves startups, small businesses, and established companies, with a particular focus on international service-based businesses. These clients need a website that reflects how their specific business works, communicates their value clearly, and helps turn visitors into inquiries or customers.

## Product Purpose

Flevio designs and develops custom websites, landing pages, portfolios, and web applications. The product exists to give businesses a fast, search-friendly, conversion-focused web presence tailored to their goals and industry rather than forcing them into a generic template.

Success means helping a client present their business clearly online and giving prospective customers an effective path toward contact or conversion.

## Positioning

Flevio combines business understanding with fully custom design and hand-coded development. Each website is tailored to the client's industry and business needs rather than assembled from a page builder or pre-made template.

## Operating Context

- Prospective clients review Flevio's services, project examples, and showreel before making contact.
- A pricing calculator gives early estimates based on project type, size, creative direction, timeline, SEO, and content-support needs.
- Clients can initiate a conversation through email, WhatsApp, or Instagram.
- The website serves both English- and Georgian-speaking visitors.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services represented in the current product include custom website development, landing pages, web applications, portfolios, and SEO optimization.
- The current website is a bilingual English/Georgian Next.js application.
- The business is based in Tbilisi, Georgia and presents itself as available to clients in Georgia and worldwide.
- Current pricing estimates are calculated in GEL, with USD conversion support in the implementation.
- No additional product-specific accessibility standard or durable operating constraint has been confirmed.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Flevio.
- Preserve the positioning around custom design, hand-coded development, business understanding, and industry-specific solutions.
- Preserve English and Georgian support.
- The new primary brand colors are Warm White (`#EFEDE6`) and Cherry Red (`#CD0000`). The rollout begins with the desktop hero; existing sections remain unchanged until they are explicitly redesigned.
- English acquisition copy should address an international audience without restricting Flevio to Georgia. Georgian copy may reference Georgia where relevant.
- Do not fabricate customer claims, testimonials, performance benchmarks, or business results.

## Product Principles

1. Understand the client's business and industry before shaping the solution.
2. Build each experience for the client's actual goals instead of adapting a generic template.
3. Make the path from understanding to inquiry clear and purposeful.
4. Treat performance, SEO, and maintainable code as part of the product rather than after-launch add-ons.
5. Serve both Georgian and international audiences without weakening the message in either language.
